# Verified Accounts Selection Criteria

We have been following this **verification process for several months**, ensuring consistency and transparency in how verified accounts are selected within the Nuance ecosystem.

To be included in the **latest verified accounts list**, an account must have **at least 5 followers** from the [Top 100 Bittensor accounts](https://github.com/NuanceNetwork/constitution/blob/main/verified_users/twitter/bittensor_target_accounts_august_followers.csv).

This process is **performed manually**, since there is currently **no open API access** to retrieve follower data across different accounts.
The **list and methodology** are fully transparent and can be **replicated by anyone**.

We **update the list continuously**, and you can **reach out at any time** to verify the number of followers for any specific account.
We typically **process updates within 48 hours**.

---

## Origin of the Top 100 List

The original list of **Top Bittensor accounts** was compiled by analyzing **engagement metrics on the most active X (Twitter) posts mentioning Bittensor**.
This helped identify the most influential and engaged members of the Bittensor community.
You can find the **full methodology here:** [METHODOLOGY](https://github.com/NuanceNetwork/constitution/blob/new-lists-july/verified_users/twitter/methodology/get_top_bittensor_accounts.ipynb)

---

## Purpose of the Verified List

This verified accounts list is used to **set weight within the system**.
Accounts with **more followers from the Top 100** have a **larger impact in the community**, which translates into:

* **Higher weight** in evaluation and scoring
* **More influence** on the visibility and credibility of posts

In short:

> More top followers → Bigger community impact → Higher weight → More post scores.
